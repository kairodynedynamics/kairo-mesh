# Contributing

Kairo Mesh is dual licensed: AGPL-3.0-or-later for everyone, and a commercial licence for closed-source use.
To keep both possible, every contribution needs the right to be offered under both.

By opening a pull request you agree that:

1. the contribution is your own work, or you have the right to submit it;
2. it is licensed to the public under AGPL-3.0-or-later;
3. you grant Kairodyne Dynamics a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to also distribute it under other terms, including commercial ones.

Security findings: please do not open a public issue. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository.

Before sending a change, run `npm test` and, for Android, `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest` in `android/`.
